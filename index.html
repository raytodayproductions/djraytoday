Good to know

Common questions

How do I book my date?

Send an inquiry below. If I'm available, we will schedule a meeting to discuss everything. If you like what you hear, I'll send a contract and invoice. A 20% deposit secures your date.

What if we need to postpone or cancel?

A full refund can be issued in case of postponement or cancellation. The deposit is non-refundable 90 days out from the date of the event.

Do you travel?

Yes. I'm based in the Dallas–Fort Worth area and also work destination weddings. The travel fee is dependent on the location I am travelling to. Simply inquire for details.

Can we make a do-not-play list and special requests?

Absolutely. You'll get a planning form for must-plays, do-not-plays, and key moments.

Can you handle cultural traditions and multiple languages?

Yes. I've worked South Asian, Middle-Eastern, and various European weddings and love learning your traditions ahead of time so everything flows.
